## The Geometry of Risk

<p class="eyebrow">Working paper · SSRN · posted September 2026 · 34 pages</p>

**The Geometry of Risk: An Integrated Monitoring Framework for Multi-Asset Systemic Stress.** Youness Yachruti, independent researcher.

A four-layer empirical framework for diagnosing multi-asset systemic stress, combining network topology, dynamic causality, tail-risk measurement and regime classification. Applied to nine global asset classes from April 2025 to April 2026 and a 14-year history, the Absorption Ratio breaches the 0.50 threshold in all four crisis windows tested, and an out-of-sample hidden Markov model trained on 14 years identifies the April 2026 stress regime without re-estimation. The paper is a preprint and has not been peer reviewed.
